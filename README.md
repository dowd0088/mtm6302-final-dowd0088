# mtm6302-final-dowd0088

**Project title:** Fitpulse 
**Who is it for? Describe the people who will use it and when they might need it. **
Anyone who is serious about the gym and fitness in general 
**What will it help them do? What need or problem does it address? **
This should help people get in shape without all the stress of tracking steps, workouts and weight on their 
own. This helps them to have everything in one place that they can refer back too when working out and 
knowing how much weight to lift in the pursuit of getting stronger. All they should need to do is plug their 
workout progress into this app and make it their personal reference for future workouts. This app should also 
track steps if you have your phone anywhere on you.

F1: Workout Tracking 
The user can track what weight they lifted and how many sets/reps they 
did on a certain workout/machine and track it as a reference for how 
much they can lift for next time, too either keep lifting that weight, or 
lifting more for progressive overload. 

F2: Weight Tracking 
A pretty straight forward feature that users can use to track their 
weight, whether its daily, weekly, bi-weekly, or however they want to 
use it. 

F3: Step Counter 
Another straight forward feature that lets users track their steps daily.
